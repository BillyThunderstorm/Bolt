# Brand And Logo Vision - Billy Carter / Bolt

This document preserves the brand vision that was sitting on the Desktop as `BRAND_VISION_DESCRIPTION.md`.

## Core Image

The logo/brand mark is a silhouetted figure with massive, full-spectrum rainbow wings spread wide open. The figure is small, almost anonymous, which is intentional. The wings are the statement. They dwarf the person beneath them, suggesting that the potential, creativity, and reach are bigger than one individual.

The wings run the full color spectrum: red and orange at the tips fading through yellow, green, blue, and violet at the base. Every color is included.

## Visual Tension

The branding lives in a deliberate contrast between two worlds:

- Heavenly/celestial: golden light, clouds, warmth, divine energy, something bigger than you.
- Urban/electric: dark city canyons, neon glow, fire, lightning, motion, hustle.

The silhouette with rainbow wings exists in both worlds equally. This is not a brand that lives in one lane.

## Lightning And Storm Element

The name Bolt is connected to electric energy. The brand can use electric blue wings, fiery skies, and lightning to connect the rainbow warmth with power and motion.

Storm imagery suggests force, arrival, and transformation. The rainbow makes it beautiful. The lightning makes it real.

## Color Palette

| Element | Color Direction |
|---|---|
| Wings | Full rainbow spectrum: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, violet |
| Backgrounds | Deep navy, charcoal, moody red, night-city darks |
| Electric accents | Bright white lightning, electric blue |
| Warm accents | Amber, gold, fire orange |
| Contrast | The wings should feel like the light source against dark backgrounds |

## Logo Mark

Primary mark:

- dark back-facing silhouette
- arms slightly out
- full rainbow wings spread wide
- transparent or very dark background
- figure grounded at bottom center
- wings fill the frame

Simplified/icon version:

- just the wings as a symmetrical emblem
- usable as avatar, watermark, favicon, or small badge

Lightning version:

- subtle electric blue lightning in the wing tips or background
- direct connection to the Bolt name

## What This Brand Says

- I contain multitudes.
- I am both heaven and street.
- There is power here, not just beauty.
- The creator behind this keeps moving even while learning.
- Everyone belongs in the spectrum.

## What This Brand Is Not

- cold or corporate
- one-dimensional
- only gamer aesthetic
- loud for the sake of loud
- exclusive or intimidating

## Brand Versus Bolt

This mark represents Billy, the channel, and the creative space.

Bolt, the AI assistant character, lives within this world. Bolt is related to the brand but is not the logo itself. Think of the wings as the studio mark and Bolt as the warm, witty, golden teammate inside that studio.
