---

## North star

**Post TikToks that feel like “Billy & Randy’s nerdy life + exclusive stuff,” often enough that money and pride can show up later — without a second job’s worth of stress.**

“Important” = a real craft, a path toward bills and pride, not clout theater.

---

## Why this plan (not the old one)

The old calendar stacked creator ops + growth + monetization + business setup for a year.  
That was a board of directors for a company you don’t run yet.

**This plan has one job:** build a **pattern**. Money follows pattern. Pattern unlocks more hours.

---

## The only loop (repeat forever)

```
Find / make vertical clip
        ↓
   1 clear hook
        ↓
  Post to TikTok
  (trending sound is fine — audio help, not failure)
        ↓
  Note what happened
        ↓
       Next
```

---

## Time budget (4–5 hours / week)

| Block | Time | What |
|--------|------|------|
| **A. Bolt day + queue** | 45–60 min, 1–2× / week | `bolt day --decide` — approve keepers, hold junk with a reason |
| **B. Post pack** | 60–90 min, 1–2× / week | Finish 1–2 verticals, on-screen hook, post TikTok |
| **C. Light learn** | 20–30 min, 1× / week | Watch *your* last 3 posts. One note: “hook worked” or “too slow” |
| **D. Randy / joy** | Protected | Nerdy thing together first. Clip second (or never). Joy is fuel. |

Later, if energy is high: **same loop, more reps** — not a new system.

---

## 30 days, week by week

### Week 1 — Clear the fog
- **Goal:** 2 posts live + an honest queue  
- `bolt day --decide` at least twice  
- Post 2 TikToks (gaming / unbox / “just got my hands on…” is fine)  
- Notes one-liner: *What felt easiest about posting?*

### Week 2 — Same loop, one upgrade
- **Goal:** 3 posts  
- Upgrade **only one** thing:  
  - on-screen **hook text** in the first second, **or**  
  - **shorter** (under ~25s if you tend long)  
- Do **not** fix audio, full branding, or a “personal brand bible”

### Week 3 — Make it “us”
- **Goal:** 3 posts  
- At least **1** is clearly Billy + Randy / exclusive / nerd-trip energy  
  (dual reaction, early access, gift for two, “Randy and I argue about…”)  
- That’s your edge vs random gaming clips

### Week 4 — Prove the pattern exists
- **Goal:** 3 posts + 10-minute review  
- Write 5 lines: best post · worst post · repeat · never again · next month’s only focus  
- Optional later: `bolt stats` / TikTok token — **after** posts exist, not instead of them  

### End of 30 days = success if:
You posted roughly **10–12 TikToks on purpose**, you know the loop, and it feels like **work with a shape** — not a dream you’re failing.

Money might not show up in 30 days. **Direction** should. That’s what unlocks 8–12 hours.

## Not for these 30 days

- Twitch schedule on the calendar  
- Multi-platform every post  
- Affiliate / brand outreach quotas  
- “Hit X followers by date”  
- Fake milestone calendars  
- Perfect audio  

**Embarrassment antidote:** post → learn one small thing → post again.  
Silence keeps the fear loud.

## The 30-day contract

1. **Channel:** TikTok only (primary).  
2. **Cadence:** 3 posts/week when possible; floor = 2.  
3. **Hours:** 4–5, same loop.  
4. **Flavor:** gaming + exclusive / nerd life with Randy when it fits.  
5. **Fear response:** post anyway; improve one small thing next time.  
6. **Win condition:** pattern first — paycheck is the sequel.
