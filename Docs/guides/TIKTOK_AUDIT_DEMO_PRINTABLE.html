Form copy (paste into TT4D)

What the app does

Bolt is a creator tool for original gameplay highlights. Creators review clips, edit captions, and publish to TikTok with full control over privacy, interactions, and commercial content disclosure.

How Content Posting is used

After OAuth, creators open Post to TikTok, which loads creator_info, requires manual privacy selection, interactions off by default, commercial disclosure per guidelines, legal consent, then publishes and polls status.
