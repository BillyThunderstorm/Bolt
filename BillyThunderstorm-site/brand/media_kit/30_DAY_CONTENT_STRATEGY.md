# Billy's 30-Day Content Strategy
**Goal:** Build a sustainable, trust-based creator brand that attracts partnerships and sponsorships.  
**Timeline:** 30 days (April 30 – May 30, 2026)  
**Status:** Active

---

## Why This Matters

You have 70 Twitch followers. You're streaming at 10pm–2am to an audience that probably doesn't exist yet. But you're *creating content*—skincare reviews, tech reviews, real beginner perspective. That's the hard part. Right now, you're only visible during those 4-hour windows.

This strategy isn't about becoming a posting machine. It's about **being findable** between streams, so when someone discovers your TikTok or Twitter, they see you're consistent, helpful, and worth following.

The long game: Sponsorships and partnerships come from trust, not follower count. Brands want to work with creators who have a *loyal* audience, not a *big* audience. You're building that now.

---

## Your Content Pillars (What You Actually Create)

1. **Skincare for Overwhelmed Beginners**
   - "I tried X routine for a week, here's what happened"
   - Product reviews from a beginner's perspective (not influencer hype)
   - Problem → solution stories (acne, sensitivity, dryness, etc.)

2. **Tech for Non-Tech People**
   - "Is this gadget worth the money?" (honest, no BS)
   - Tech tips for streamers, creators, or everyday use
   - Setup tours, workflow optimization

3. **Creator / Self-Taught Journey**
   - Building Bolt publicly (what you learned, why you built it, how it works)
   - Learning PyTorch, neural networks, AI (your journey, not a tutorial)
   - Failures and fixes (because that's what real learning looks like)

4. **Stream Clips & Highlights**
   - Funny moments, "aha" moments, impressive moments from your streams
   - Bolt auto-generates these — you just need to post them
   - These are your *highest ROI content* (already recorded, just repurposed)

## Platform Strategy (Where to Post)

### Twitch (Your Home Base)
- Stream schedule: **Tues–Fri, 10pm–2am**
- Goal: Consistent 90-min streams (not 4 hours, just focus)
- Content: Live skincare reviews, tech reviews, occasional Bolt talk
- Call-to-action: "I post clips from these streams on TikTok and Twitter. Follow there too."

### TikTok (Highest Growth Potential)
- **Post 3x per week** (Mon, Wed, Fri) — NOT daily, just consistent
- 15–60 second clips from streams (via Bolt's vertical_clips/)
- Quick reviews, quick tips, funny moments, learning moments
- Best TikTok content: relatable problems, simple solutions, real person (you)
- Goal: 5–10K followers by end of Q2 → sponsorship conversations

### Twitter / X (For Niche & Connection)
- **Post 4–5x per week** (daily or near-daily)
- Thoughts on what you're learning (Bolt, Python, AI)
- Skincare/tech takes (hot takes, contrarian reviews, what worked for you)
- Engage with 5–10 other creators daily (comment, retweet, build community)
- Goal: Become known as "the beginner skincare + tech reviewer who's real"

### YouTube Shorts (Optional, Start Week 2)
- Repurpose TikToks here (they go both places)
- Same 15–60 second clips
- Goal: Cross-platform presence, algorithmic coverage

### Discord (Membership/Community)
- Create free Discord server (invite link on all platforms)
- Post clips there first, get early feedback
- Build community of people interested in skincare + tech + self-taught coding
- This is where sponsorships + partnerships start (community proof)

### Email (Week 3 Onward)
- Start collecting emails via Substack (free tier)
- Weekly email: "Here's what I learned this week" (3–4 min read)
- Goal: Direct audience relationship, not dependent on algorithm

## How Bolt Powers This Strategy

**The Pipeline:**
1. You stream (Twitch, 10pm–2am, Tues–Fri)
2. Bolt records and detects highlights (good moments, clear audio)
3. Bolt generates clips (short, punchy, auto-subtitled)
4. You post clips to TikTok (3x/week) + Twitter (share links)
5. Twitter drive engagement, TikTok drives follower growth
6. Followers land on your Discord or Twitch
7. Repeat

**In practice:**
- Monday morning: Check `Peak_Hour_Notifier` for new clips
- Post best clip to TikTok (copy caption from Bolt's `Title_Generator`)
- Share clip link on Twitter with added commentary
- Respond to comments for 30 min
- Done for the day

**This is why Bolt matters:** Without it, you'd need to manually edit clips. With it, you just *post*. That's the 10X difference.
