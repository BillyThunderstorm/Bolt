Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

About Bolt

Bolt is a personal creator workflow tool created and operated solely by its developer (Billy Carter) for their own personal use. It is not a commercial product and is not available to the general public. Bolt exists to help a single creator process, organize, and publish their own content.

Data Collection

Bolt does not collect, store, or share personal data from any third-party users. The application is used exclusively by its developer to manage their own streaming and video content on platforms including TikTok, Twitch, and related services. Bolt has no end-users other than the developer who operates it.

TikTok Data Usage

Bolt accesses the TikTok Content Posting API solely to:

  • Upload video content created by the developer to the developer's own TikTok account
  • Read basic account information for the developer's own account
  • Save draft queue items locally for the developer's own review before posting

Bolt does not auto-post content without explicit developer action. All posts are reviewed by the developer before publication, either manually or via explicit approval commands. No TikTok data is shared with any third parties.

Data Storage

Any data used by Bolt is stored locally on the developer's personal computer in plain-text configuration files (such as .env) and is not transmitted to any external servers beyond the official APIs of the platforms being accessed (TikTok, Twitch, ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Discord, etc.). Bolt runs entirely on the developer's local machine and does not operate any public-facing servers.

Third-Party Services

Bolt interacts with the following third-party platforms using their official APIs and in accordance with their respective terms of service:

  • TikTok (content publishing via the official Content Posting API)
  • Twitch (stream monitoring and chat, via the official Helix API)
  • OpenAI (transcription and language-model features, only when explicitly enabled)
  • ElevenLabs (text-to-speech voice output, only when explicitly enabled)
  • Discord (notification webhooks, only when configured)
  • OBS Studio (local broadcast software, via the local WebSocket API)

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as Bolt's features change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
