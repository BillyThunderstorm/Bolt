# Short-Form Strategy

## Current Platforms

| Platform | Role |
|---|---|
| TikTok | primary short clips, highlights, quick takes |
| Twitch | live source material and real-time personality |
| Instagram / X | repurposed clips and social presence |
| YouTube | future long-form reviews and playthroughs |

## Core Strategy

Clip everything from Twitch, but do not make everything feel like a Twitch leftover.

Each short-form post should have one clear job:

- show a good gameplay moment
- show an honest reaction
- test a product/game feature
- explain something Billy learned
- invite people into the learning-in-public story

## Voice Rules

- Real talk beats hype.
- One specific point beats five vague points.
- The learning angle is part of the brand.
- If a clip needs too much explanation to work, it may be better as a longer post or a script.

## Signals To Track

When posting, log:

- platform
- title/hook
- content type
- views
- likes
- comments worth remembering
- what Billy thinks made it work or flop
