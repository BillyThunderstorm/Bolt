# AI Development Learning

## Direction

AI development is part of the content vision and part of Bolt's identity. Billy is learning how AI systems work while building Bolt as a virtual teammate that can help learn, grow, teach, and improve over time.

Bolt should remember AI lessons in a practical way: what Billy learned, how it affects Bolt, and what can be turned into content or a future feature.

## What To Track

- AI concept or tool learned
- why it matters
- how it applies to Bolt
- what was confusing
- what clicked
- experiment or feature idea
- content angle or teaching note

## Content Angles

- "I am learning how this AI concept works by building it into Bolt"
- "Here is what finally made this click"
- "What I thought AI could do vs what I learned it actually does"
- "Building a virtual teammate one feature at a time"
- "The cheap/local-first way I am trying before paid services"

## Bolt Teammate Vision

Bolt is not just a posting tool. The long-term goal is for Bolt to become a local-first virtual teammate that can:

- remember creator goals and experiments
- retrieve relevant lessons before making suggestions
- help plan content across multiple lanes
- explain decisions clearly
- learn from posted results
- support product testing, skincare testing, gaming clips, tech reviews, and AI learning

## Guardrails

- Prefer local/free tools first.
- Keep optional paid AI behind config or explicit choice.
- Do not let AI features replace practical workflow basics.
- Add learning layers sequentially so the system stays understandable.
