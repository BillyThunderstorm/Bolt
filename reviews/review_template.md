# Review Video Script Template

Use this structure for consistent, professional reviews. Adjust length based on format (short-form vs long-form).

---

## SHORT-FORM REVIEW (TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Reels)
**Target length: 30-60 seconds**

### Hook (0-5 sec)
Grab attention immediately. Show the product, make a bold claim, or ask a question.

> "This [product name] costs $[price] — is it actually worth it?"
> "I've been using the [product] for [time] — here's the honest truth."
> "Everything you need to know about the [product] in 60 seconds."

### What It Is (5-15 sec)
Quick overview — what is it, who's it for.

> "[Product name] is a [category] from [brand]. It's built for [target audience]."

### Hands-On / Demo (15-35 sec)
Show the product in action. B-roll footage, close-ups, actual usage.

> "Build quality is [impression]. [Specific feature] feels [observation]."
> "In use, [feature] works [well/poorly]. [Specific example]."

### Pros & Cons (35-45 sec)
Fast-fire the goods and bads.

> **Pros:** [pro 1], [pro 2], [pro 3]
> **Cons:** [con 1], [con 2]

### Verdict (45-55 sec)
Final recommendation. Be honest.

> "For $[price], this is [worth it / pass / depends]. If you [use case], yes. If you [use case], skip it."
> "Drop a follow for more honest reviews — link in bio if you want to grab one."

### Disclosure (if applicable)
> "#ad — thanks to [brand] for sending this out. Review is my honest opinion."

---

## LONG-FORM REVIEW (YouTube)
**Target length: 5-10 minutes**

### 1. Intro (0:00-0:30)
- Hook: why this product, why now
- Quick verdict teaser (don't give away the full conclusion)
- "This is the [product name] and today we're finding out if it's worth your money."

### 2. Unboxing & First Impressions (0:30-2:00)
- What's in the box
- Build quality, materials, first thoughts
- Packaging quality (shows brand care)

### 3. Setup & Installation (2:00-3:30)
- How easy is it to get going
- Any friction points, software issues, compatibility

### 4. Hands-On Testing (3:30-6:00)
- Actual usage footage
- Test specific features that matter to gamers
- Compare to expectations and/or competitors at same price
- Performance, comfort, durability observations

### 5. Pros & Cons (6:00-7:30)
- Clear list, visual on screen
- Be specific, not vague ("good build quality" → "aluminum frame with no flex")

### 6. Price & Value (7:30-8:30)
- Is it worth the asking price?
- What does it compete with at this price point?
- Who should buy it / who should skip it

### 7. Final Verdict (8:30-9:30)
- Clear recommendation
- Score out of 10 (optional but helps consistency)
- "If you found this helpful, subscribe — I review gaming and tech gear every week. Link in description to check it out."

### 8. Outro (9:30-10:00)
- What's coming next
- Subscribe CTA
- Affiliate link reminder

---

## Review Checklist (Before Publishing)

- [ ] Product name and model number correct
- [ ] Price mentioned is current
- [ ] Pros and cons are specific, not generic
- [ ] Footage is clear and well-lit
- [ ] Audio is clean (no echo, no background noise)
- [ ] Disclosure included if product was gifted/sponsored
- [ ] Affiliate links in description (if enrolled)
- [ ] Title is click-worthy but honest (no clickbait lies)
- [ ] Thumbnail is clear and shows the product
- [ ] Tags and hashtags relevant to product and niche

---

## Content Ideas to Start With (Stuff You Probably Already Own)

1. Your gaming headset
2. Your keyboard
3. Your mouse
4. Your monitor
5. Your phone (for gaming)
6. Any desk accessory (mousepad, wrist rest, cable management)
7. Any cheap gadget you bought that surprised you
8. Anything you bought that disappointed you (honest negative reviews perform well)

*Start with what you have. Review it. Post it. Repeat. Then pitch brands with proof you can do this.*
