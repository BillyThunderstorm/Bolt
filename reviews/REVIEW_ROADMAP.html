Golden Rules

  1. Honesty over everything. Bad reviews build trust. Shilling kills it.
  2. Consistency wins. 1-2 reviews/week + 3 clips/day = momentum.
  3. Start small. Cheap accessories are easier to get than expensive gear.
  4. Follow up. One follow-up email after 7 days, then move on.
  5. Disclose everything. #ad or “sponsored by [brand]” is non-negotiable.
  6. Track everything. Log every pitch, every product, every review in the tracker.
  7. Your audience is small but that’s fine. Engaged 2K > dead 200K. Brands care about fit and engagement.
