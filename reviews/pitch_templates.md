## Tips for Sending

- **Contact 10-20 brands at a time** — expect mostly no's
- **Find the right contact:** look for "Press," "Partnerships," "Marketing," or "Affiliates" pages on company websites. If nothing, use the general contact form.
- **Start with smaller brands and accessories** — easier yes than asking for a $200 keyboard from a major brand on day one
- **Personalize each email** — mention a specific product, reference their brand, show you actually know them
- **Attach your media kit** as a PDF (export media_kit.md to PDF)
- **Follow up once** after 7 days, then move on if no response
- **Track every pitch** in the review tracker (review_tracker.json)
