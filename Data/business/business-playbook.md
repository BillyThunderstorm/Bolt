## Stage Map

1. **Proof** — post consistent detailed product assessments (one lane: product testing)
2. **Portfolio** — 5+ public reviews + media kit
3. **Affiliate** — every relevant review uses tracked links
4. **Outreach** — pitch ~5 brands/week with templates
5. **Deals** — gifting → paid once metrics exist
6. **Systems** — Bolt automates research, drafts, queue, follow-ups

## Weekly Rhythm

- Mon: pick 1 product, copy the assessment template, list its claims (`bolt manage add "Item" --lane product --status testing`)
- Tue–Thu: test it + 5-min daily notes (`bolt manage note "Item" --day N --text "..."`)
- Fri: write verdict + score; draft the short (`bolt manage draft "Item" --format short`)
- Sat: approve + post 1 thing (approval required in Bolt). Comments optional.
- Sun: 10-min look back (`bolt log-perf`), pick next product
