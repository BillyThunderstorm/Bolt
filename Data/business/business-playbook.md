Become a trusted voice for **game and tech testing**, with product/skincare lanes as expansion.
Monetize via Amazon Influencer (`billycarter-20`), affiliates, then brand deals.

## Stage Map
1. **Proof** — post consistent honest reviews/clips (game + tech first)
2. **Portfolio** — 5+ public reviews + media kit
3. **Affiliate** — every relevant review uses tracked links
4. **Outreach** — pitch ~5 brands/week with templates
5. **Deals** — gifting → paid once metrics exist
6. **Systems** — Bolt automates research, drafts, queue, follow-ups

## Weekly Rhythm
- Mon: pick 1 game or tech item to test / film
- Tue: journal notes + short-form cut
- Wed: post + engage comments (approval required in Bolt)
- Thu: long-form or stream segment
- Fri: pitch brands / check Amazon dashboard
- Sat: Twitch stream + clip harvest
- Sun: review numbers + plan next week with Bolt (`bolt morning`)

## Disclosures
- Always disclose gifted/sponsored content (#ad / #gifted)
- Affiliate links need honest opinion first

## First Money Paths
1. Amazon Associates / Influencer (`billycarter-20`)
2. Platform creator funds
3. Brand affiliate programs (peripherals, stream gear)
4. Sponsored reviews after portfolio exists

## Advancement Rule
Ship content before perfect systems. Bolt upgrades should reduce friction on posting and testing.
