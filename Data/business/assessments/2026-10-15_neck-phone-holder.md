# Assessment: K&F CONCEPT 2-in-1 Magnetic & Clip-on Neck Phone Holder

- **Price paid / where bought / date:** $23.99 · Amazon · 2026-07-19
- **Category:** General
- **Claims tested:**
  1. Switch between portrait and landscape instantly by rotating 90 degrees, with no attachment changes.
  2. Hang it around your neck for stable, hands-free shooting.
  3. Air cushion plus an adjustable 1.5 m chest strap greatly reduce shake even while running and jumping; fits people up to 75 in / 190 cm comfortably for long use.
- **Test method:** Recorded 2 content videos: my nightly skincare routine, and a basic workout (push-ups, sit-ups, light jog).
  1. Rotation: not the smoothest transition ever, but the best I've come across. With a non-Pro Max phone it would probably work perfectly.
  2. Ease of use: everything is simple and stress-relieving except the chest strap. Stability really depends on the chest piece, not the neck holder, and it was hard to fit comfortably.
  3. Stability and comfort: the chest strap should be the selling point for stability. The neck air cushion is needed for extended use and beats most other phone holders. The chest fit still has kinks, and sharing it means a short delay while re-adjusting straps. Not nearly enough to call it a bad product; it's fantastic.
- **Duration:** about 3 months / frequent use
- **Day-by-day notes:** _(not recorded)_
- **Measurable results:** Everything worked across 3 months of frequent use; the only friction was fitting the chest strap.
- **Pros:** Everything works, easy to attach, holds the phone great.
- **Cons:** Chest strap is hard to fit comfortably.
- **Who it's for:** Active, hands-free recorders.
- **Who it's NOT for:** Thinner-built people may have the same chest strap fit issue I did.
- **Verdict + score (8/10):** Highly recommend. Almost a necessity for active content creators.
- **Disclosure:** Bought with my own money.
