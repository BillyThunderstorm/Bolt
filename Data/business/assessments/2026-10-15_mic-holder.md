# Assessment: Facmogu Pink Mic Holder (handle for DJI Wireless Mic / Rode Wireless Pro)

- **Price paid / where bought / date:** $17.09 · Amazon · 2026-09-30
- **Category:** General / Tech
- **Claims tested:**
  1. Slot design with an added iron plate (for magnetic clips) that securely holds most wireless lavalier mics.
  2. 3 premium windshield foams give a secure fit and reduce wind and ambient noise for clearer recordings.
  3. Turns a lavalier mic into a professional handheld mic for interviews, live streams, podcasts, and video content.
- **Test method:** Recorded, live streamed, and FaceTimed with the mic in the holder.
- **Duration:** 14 days / 5 uses
- **Day-by-day notes:**
  - Day 1: Cute but difficult to hear.
  - Day 3: Very precise aiming needed, but that's probably the mic.
  - Day 7: Eh. Sure?
- **Measurable results:** I wouldn't rave about it, but it isn't bad.
- **Pros:** Holds up outside.
- **Cons:** Childish in a sense.
- **Who it's for:** If you need a mic holder and didn't buy a name-brand one, this works.
- **Who it's NOT for:** It can come off as a prop, so seriously consider that it's a hot pink microphone.
- **Verdict + score (7/10):** It works for me, but I can easily see why it wouldn't work for others. If it isn't pointed right at what it needs to hear, you probably won't catch it. Every comment I got mentioned how cute it was, which almost made me feel like what I was doing wasn't taken seriously. The product isn't bad, but without having tried anything else, I'm convinced I could find something more effective. It does everything it claims, I guess. It's definitely a conversation starter, however awkward it turns out.
- **If it's a dud, what works instead:** Not a dud.
- **Disclosure:** Bought with my own money.
