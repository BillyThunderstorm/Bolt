## 2. Commander's pitch

**Mission: Profit Positively**  
**Core direction (lock this before any execution):** Build a small, trustworthy engine that turns honest local-first reviews of gear you actually own/use into affiliate revenue (Amazon tag `billycarter-20`) and eventual sponsorships—without gimmicks, fake hype, or posting pressure that burns you out. Profit = revenue consistently above your real costs (time, any gear, tools). Early stage means we prioritize proof-of-concept cashflow and audience signals over scale.

You are local-first, honesty-first, owned-gear-first. That is the moat. People buy from creators who feel real and specific, not from hype accounts. We will not chase viral gimmicks or review products you don’t have/use. Every piece of content should answer: “Would I trust this recommendation with my own money?”

### 1. Profit Model (Simple & Aligned)
Primary near-term: **Amazon Influencer / affiliate** via tag `billycarter-20`.  
- Review or feature owned tech, games-related gear, and later skincare/products you actually use.  
- Drive traffic from TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Twitch to your storefront or specific ASIN links.  
- Secondary (later): Twitch bits/subs, YouTube RTS/affiliates, brand sponsorships once you have consistent posting + small proof of trust.  

Avoid: Paid gimmick products, undisclosed stuff, or anything that forces you to perform a persona that isn’t you.

Success metrics (start tiny):  
- 1–2 affiliate clicks or sales per week within 30–45 days of consistent posting.  
- Content that feels sustainable (you’re not dreading it).  
- Clear tracking: note what you posted, which link, rough views/clicks.

### 2. Positioning & Content Pillars (Direction)
- **Who you are on camera/page:** Self-taught creator figuring it out in public. Honest real-world testing of games + tech gear you own. No screaming hype. “Here’s what actually happened when I used it.”  
- **Primary pillars this season:**  
  1. Games + tech gear (owned controllers, headsets, keyboards, monitors, accessories, game-related hardware).  
  2. Quick honest takes / “after X hours” updates.  
  3. Light expansion: skincare or general products only if you already use and can review honestly.  
- **Formats that convert without gimmicks:** Short demo + real opinion + “link in bio/storefront if you want the same one.” Twitch can be longer gameplay + occasional gear talk.  
- **Local-first angle:** Film in your real setup, mention real conditions (lighting, desk, how it feels after a session). Specificity builds trust faster than polish.

### 3. 30-Day Mission Plan (Phased)
**Phase 0 – Direction lock (Do this first, 1 sitting):**  
- List every piece of owned gear/games-related product you can honestly review right now (headset, mouse, keyboard, chair, capture stuff, specific games, skincare you use, etc.). Pick your top 5–7 that have Amazon listings.  
- Confirm Amazon Influencer access and that tag `billycarter-20` is ready. Create or clean a simple storefront with those items.  
- Decide your posting minimum: 3–5 short pieces/week total across TikTok + YouTube Shorts (repurpose the same clip). Twitch 1x/week if energy allows. Quality of honesty > quantity.  
- One-sentence content filter: “Would I tell a friend to buy this with my actual experience?” If no, skip.

**Phase 1 – Foundation (Days 1–10): Make the engine real**  
- Batch-record 5–8 short honest clips from your owned list (60–90 sec): show the item, one real pro, one real con or limitation, who it’s actually for, soft CTA to storefront/link.  
- Post the first 3. Use simple captions. Same video can go TikTok + YouTube Shorts + tease on X.  
- On Twitch (if streaming): one natural gear mention per session with link in panels.  
- Set up basic tracking (notes app or spreadsheet): Date / Platform / Product / Link / Views / Any clicks or feedback.  
- Why this order: You need live links and real posts before optimizing. Direction → ship.

**Phase 2 – Feedback & Tightening (Days 11–20):**  
- Review what got any watch time or questions. Double down on the style that felt least fake to you.  
- Add 2–3 more clips. Include one “update after more use” if possible—builds credibility.  
- Make sure every description/bio has the Amazon storefront or key links.  
- One improvement only: clearer lighting/audio or tighter first 3 seconds. No tool rabbit holes.  
- Start light outreach mindset: engage genuine comments; save any brand-looking DMs for later.

**Phase 3 – Profit signal (Days 21–30):**  
- Aim for first measurable affiliate activity (even 1 sale or consistent clicks).  
- Package your best performer into a simple “honest review” longer cut if YouTube long-form energy appears.  
- Review numbers: What content drove interest? What drained you? Adjust pillars or frequency.  
- Decide next 30-day target (e.g., 4 products live, first $X affiliate, or first sponsorship convo).  

### 4. Guardrails (So You Don’t Stall)
- **Posting fear / analysis paralysis:** Pre-decide the next 3 products and shoot them in one session. Ship imperfect. The best content is the content that goes out.  
- **Overwhelm:** One clear next action per day max. I will always give you a single recommended next step when you check in.  
- **Motivation with low views:** Track effort and honesty, not just views. Early profit comes from trust + links, not virality. Celebrate the first click.  
- **Costs:** Keep near zero. Use what you own. No new gear “for content” until affiliate covers it.  
- **Disclosure:** Always be clear it’s affiliate/your real experience. Protects trust and keeps you platform-safe.  
- **No gimmicks:** If an idea feels like clickbait or forces a fake reaction, kill it.
