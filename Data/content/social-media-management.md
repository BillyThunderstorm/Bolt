# Social Media Management

## Direction

Cross-platform presence without burnout. Bolt helps schedule, post, track, and learn from social content across TikTok, Twitch, Instagram, X, and eventually YouTube.

The goal is consistent output, not overwhelming volume. One platform at a time, one post at a time.

## What To Track

- Post queue: content reference, platform, status, intended time
- Peak hours per platform (estimated or data-driven)
- Posted-content log with performance notes
- Hashtag and caption templates per platform
- Community engagement reminders

## Queue Management

Queue tiers:
- **Ready**: formatted, captioned, approved — waiting for the right time
- **Pending**: needs one more step (caption, format, Billy approval)
- **Idea**: not yet recorded or created

Bolt surfaces one ready item at a time with a recommended platform and time.

## Peak Hour Awareness

Platform timing (default estimates if no data):
- TikTok: evening (6-9 PM local)
- Instagram: lunch or evening (12-1 PM, 7-9 PM)
- X: morning or late evening (8-10 AM, 9-11 PM)
- YouTube: weekend afternoons (future)

Adjust based on actual performance data as it becomes available.

## Post Formatting

Per-platform voice:
- TikTok: short, punchy, hook-first, 3-5 hashtags
- Instagram: slightly longer, emoji-friendly, story-style
- X: direct, opinion-forward, thread-friendly
- YouTube: descriptive, keyword-rich, chapter-ready

Captions must sound like Billy, not generic marketing copy.

## Cross-Post Coordination

Rules:
- Do not blast identical content everywhere at once
- Stagger by 2-6 hours per platform
- Repurpose rather than duplicate (re-cut, re-caption, re-contextualize)
- Respect platform culture (what works on TikTok may flop on X)

## Analytics Notes

Log what was posted, when, and any visible results. Do not require API access.
Format:
```
Platform: TikTok
Content: clip-2026-06-08-highlight-3.mp4
Time: 7:15 PM
Views: 1,200 (estimated)
Notes: strong hook, comments asked about the game
Lesson: show the game title in first 2 seconds
```

## Community Touch

Remind Billy to respond to comments or DMs on high-engagement posts.
Frequency: within 24 hours of posting.

## Guardrails

- Never post without Billy approval unless explicitly configured otherwise.
- Captions must sound like Billy.
- One platform at a time per suggestion.
- Respect rate limits and platform rules.
- No "post everywhere" dumps.
