# Product Reviews

## Direction

Billy wants to become a recognized voice in gaming highlights, tech reviews, general product testing, and practical recommendations. Product reviews connect directly to his Amazon Influencer storefront, so Bolt should remember products, tests, verdicts, and audience usefulness.

The review style should be:

- honest
- real-world
- clear about what worked and what did not
- useful to someone deciding whether to buy or try something
- grounded in Billy's actual experience, not hype

## Review Voice

Avoid fake excitement and generic sponsor language.

Lean into:

- "Here is what I actually noticed"
- "This confused me at first"
- "This is who I think this is for"
- "This is where it surprised me"
- "This is what I would change"

## Amazon Influencer Storefront Context

Reviews should help someone decide whether a product is actually worth trying or buying. Bolt should track:

- product name and category
- where it fits in the storefront
- why Billy tested it
- real-world use case
- what worked
- what did not work
- who it is for
- honest verdict
- content links or posting results when available

## Reusable Review Shape

1. What it is
2. Why Billy tried it
3. First impression
4. What worked
5. What got in the way
6. Who it is for
7. Honest verdict
