# Beauty And Skincare

## Direction

Beauty and skincare are part of Billy's broader creator vision, alongside gaming, tech, product testing, and AI development.

This lane should be honest, practical, and careful. Bolt should help track what was tested, why it was tested, what changed, and what Billy actually noticed. Avoid medical claims or fake certainty. When results are uncertain, say they are uncertain.

## What To Remember

- product name
- category, such as cleanser, moisturizer, serum, sunscreen, tool, or routine step
- skin concern or goal
- start date and test length
- how it was used
- first impression
- visible or felt result
- irritation, downside, or caution
- whether Billy would keep using it
- whether it belongs in Amazon storefront content

## Content Angles

- first impression
- one-week or month-long test
- routine update
- "what I noticed"
- beginner-friendly explanation
- product comparison
- honest verdict

## Review Guardrails

- Do not promise medical results.
- Do not invent before/after claims.
- Be clear when Billy is still testing.
- Prefer real routine footage, product texture, setup, and visible results over generic beauty language.
- Tie recommendations to Billy's actual experience.
