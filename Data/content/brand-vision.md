# Brand Vision Memory

This memory summarizes `brand/BRAND_VISION_DESCRIPTION.md` so Bolt can retrieve the brand direction when planning content, titles, site copy, visuals, or identity work.

## Core Direction

Billy's brand mark is a silhouetted figure with massive full-spectrum rainbow wings. The wings are the statement: creativity, reach, inclusion, and potential bigger than one person.

The visual world combines two energies:

- celestial warmth, golden light, and something bigger than you
- urban/electric motion, city darkness, fire, lightning, and hustle

## What Bolt Should Remember

- The wings belong to Billy/the channel/the creative space.
- Bolt is the teammate or character inside that world, not the whole logo.
- The brand is not only gaming, not only tech, and not only AI.
- The rainbow spectrum supports the idea that the creator contains multitudes.
- Lightning connects the name Bolt to power, motion, and energy.

## Content Uses

- explain the brand story in a short-form post
- guide logo and site decisions
- keep product, skincare, gaming, AI, and Bolt-building content under one creative identity
- avoid cold corporate design language
- avoid reducing the brand to one lane
